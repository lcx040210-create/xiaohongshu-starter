# Xiaohongshu Account Starter

Use this skill when the user wants to start a Xiaohongshu account from zero and turn it into a working business channel in about one month. It teaches a reusable workflow in the agent's own words. Read the linked original tutorial for its full examples and wording. Do not copy its text into a deliverable.

## Define the business before the account

- Start from what a buyer asks before buying and write the offer in concrete terms.

- Split the revenue model into separate lines such as brand work, product sales, referral commission and personal service and check the cost and platform rule for each line before promising it.

- Write positioning as one executable sentence that names the audience stage, the concrete scene, the problem and the material or ability used to explain it.

- Judge vertical focus by whether the content serves the same group of people, not by whether the topics look similar.

- Build the persona from real choices and real work. Do not stack identities in the bio.

## Learn from comparable accounts

- Collect 5 to 10 comparable accounts that answer the same kind of user question.

- Record three lines for each account covering who it helps, what makes its content recognisable and what single point to learn from it.

- Judge an account from several posts. Do not treat one high like count as proof of commercial success.

- Compare accounts with similar conditions, not only similar follower counts.

## Build a topic bank from real questions

- Collect questions in the asker's own wording from search phrases, comments on comparable posts, service conversations and work notes.

- Give each topic a target reader, an answer format, the material already on hand, the material still missing and the next step you want the reader to take.

- Start once you have a dozen or so concrete questions. Do not wait for a perfect bank.

- Rank topics by what you can actually produce now, not by what looks most popular.

## Prepare the homepage and the first batch together

- Make the nickname easy to remember and the avatar clear at small size.

- Answer three questions in the bio covering who you are, who you help and why the account is worth following.

- Use pinned posts for different jobs such as introduction, proof of ability and common questions.

- Plan a first batch of about six posts that answer questions, show process and state the conditions for choosing a solution.

## Produce one note with a clear job

- Draft the body outline before the title and check the title back against the body.

- Give the cover one main job and let it complement the title instead of repeating it.

- Give every page in a carousel a job of its own.

- End with one natural next step for the reader. Do not ask for everything at once.

- Choose image text or video by what must be shown. Use image text for comparison and reference and video for action and process.

- Use AI to organise material only when the evidence comes from the user or a verified source. Read the draft aloud and cut stock phrases.

## Check before publishing and review by stage

- Check title and body consistency, image readability, permission for material used and whether every factual claim can be explained.

- Pick a publishing rhythm the user can sustain and keep a version record for each post.

- Answer real questions in comments first and correct misunderstandings promptly.

- Split results into stages such as exposure, reads, interaction, profile visits, follows, inquiries and orders and diagnose the weak stage instead of judging the whole post as good or bad.

- Keep one explicit next change from each review. Do not draw conclusions from a tiny sample and keep the measurement definition the same across comparisons.

- Define a qualified inquiry before selling. It needs a concrete need, a match with what can be supplied and a willingness to confirm a next step.

- Describe delivery in concrete steps that are more specific than a slogan and use only the contact route the account is currently allowed to use on the platform.

## Sequence paid promotion, tools and expansion

- State what a paid test is meant to buy and how much test cost is acceptable before spending.

- Record organic and paid results separately.

- Get one account workflow smooth before expanding to more accounts.

- Add a tool only when it removes a specific bottleneck in the current workflow.

## Run a four week execution loop

- Week one prepares direction material covering the business card, positioning, comparable accounts, topic list and sample drafts.

- Week two publishes a varied first set and records the production time for each piece.

- Week three picks one piece of feedback and improves it in depth.

- Week four decides what to continue and what to stop. Present this as an execution example, not as a promise of revenue.

## Deliverables to leave behind

- Leave a positioning card, a comparable account card, a single post production card and a review and inquiry card.

- Each card must be usable without the agent. A card that only makes sense in one conversation is unfinished.

- For an AI generation or membership business, add a separate risk line to the positioning card covering platform transfer rules, account stability and refund handling. Do not promise permanent access for a resold account.
